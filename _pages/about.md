I am Noor Haddad, a first-year PhD candidate at Delta Language Analytics, a research group that is part of Delta Science University and is led by Professor Omar Khalid. Professor Omar Khalid is my PhD supervisor. My research focuses on natural language processing and machine learning, and my research interests include Reinforcement Learning for Reasoning, Hallucination in Vision-Language Models, and Interpretability of Truthfulness.
